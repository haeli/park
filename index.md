# Hae-Li Park

<p class="lead">
Research Professor, Ewha Womans University
</p>

I am an atmospheric scientist studying large-scale atmospheric and climate
dynamics. My work focuses on what shapes winter climate variability over the
Northern Hemisphere and East Asia, including the East Asian Winter Monsoon,
and on how that understanding can improve seasonal prediction.

I received my Ph.D. in Atmospheric Science from Pusan National University in
2022.

## Research interests

- Large-scale atmospheric dynamics
- Atmospheric and climate dynamics
- East Asian Winter Monsoon
- Air–sea interaction
- Climate change

Read more on the [Research](research.md) page, or see my
[Publications](publications.md) and [CV](cv.md).
