# Research

I study large-scale atmospheric and climate dynamics, combining observational
analysis with numerical modeling.

## East Asian Winter Monsoon

The East Asian Winter Monsoon controls cold-season weather and climate across
East Asia. My current work looks at the large-scale circulation behind its
variability.

## Wintertime surface air temperature variability

Winter surface air temperature over the Northern Hemisphere extratropics varies
in a few dominant, recurring patterns. I have worked on identifying these modes
and the mechanisms behind them, on the physical factors that form the
associated sea level pressure patterns, and on the roles of Eurasian snow cover
and sea ice.

- Park, H.-L., K.-H. Seo, B.-M. Kim, J.-Y. Kim, and S.-Y. S. Wang, 2021:
  Dominant wintertime surface air temperature modes in the Northern Hemisphere
  extratropics. *Climate Dynamics*.

## Seasonal prediction of the Changma onset

The Changma is the Korean rainy season within the East Asian summer monsoon.
I developed a dynamics-based statistical model that predicts the year-to-year
timing of its onset from sea surface temperature anomalies.

- Park, H.-L., K.-H. Seo, and J.-H. Son, 2015: Development of a dynamics-based
  statistical prediction model for the Changma onset. *Journal of Climate*,
  **28**, 6647–6666.
